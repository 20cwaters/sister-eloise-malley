The short version

She loves hearing from people

How to write her

Email — the easy way

Missionaries get one preparation day a week, and that is when they read and answer email. Write any day you like; she will see it on her next P-day.

Email her

Letters and packages

Physical mail goes to the mission office, and they pass it along at transfers or zone conferences, so give it a week or two to reach her. Missionaries move around, so the mission office is the safest address for anything you post — and for packages, small and light travels best.

A few tips. Keep it upbeat and newsy — she only gets a few hours on P-day to read and reply. Tell her what is happening at home, ask about the people she is teaching, and send pictures. Do not be discouraged if the reply is short; P-day goes quickly.
